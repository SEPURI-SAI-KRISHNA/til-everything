# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Being respectful and considerate in communication
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Personal attacks, trolling, or insulting language
- Publishing others’ private information without consent

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the maintainer through repository contact channels.
